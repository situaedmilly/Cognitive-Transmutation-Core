# OURSELF Sovereign Technology Doctrine
## Doctrine ID: OURSELF-SOVEREIGN-TECHNOLOGY-001
## Status: Governing doctrine / qualification required

### 1. Purpose

Establish OURSELF as the authority, technological estate, and governing institution for its own cognitive and execution realities. The objective is to engineer the ability to originate, operate, reproduce, replace, and govern the capabilities required for an independently controlled cognitive-technology ecosystem.

### 2. Governing ontology

- **OURSELF OPENAI** is the name of OURSELF's sovereign cognitive-technology reality. It does not assert ownership of OpenAI Inc., its corporate identity, or its proprietary models.
- **OURSELF MODELS** are models whose architecture, tokenizer, training lineage, weights, evaluation, distribution, and custody are governed and evidenced by OURSELF.
- **OURSELF COMPUTE** is computational infrastructure over which OURSELF has independently evidenced administrative and operational control.
- **OURSELF CONTROL** governs identity, founder authority, jurisdiction, admission, memory, routing, capabilities, and receipts.
- **OURSELF EXECUTION** comprises independently governed execution realities, including GBTSELF and HTMLSELF.
- **OURSELF KNOWLEDGE** comprises OURSELF-controlled data, knowledge graphs, institutional memory, provenance, and retention policy.
- **SOVEREIGN QUALIFICATION** is the evidence-based process by which a component or reality earns a bounded sovereignty claim.

External products and services may be studied as engineering references and used during a declared transition period. They are not the permanent source of OURSELF's cognitive authority.

### 3. Sovereignty is a qualification, not a label

Local execution alone does not establish sovereignty. Downloading third-party weights does not establish model origin or ownership. An OURSELF-controlled interface does not establish control of the inference engine beneath it.

Every sovereignty claim MUST identify its scope, custody boundary, external dependencies, evidence, and unresolved qualifications.

### 4. Capability domains

1. **Control plane:** founder identity, authorization, jurisdiction, capability policy, admission, audit, and receipts.
2. **Inference engine:** model loading, scheduling, inference serving, resource management, isolation, observability, and reproducible deployment.
3. **Model estate:** model architecture, tokenizer, training data governance, training pipeline, weights, evaluation, versioning, and custody.
4. **Cognition:** reasoning, conversation, memory, multimodal processing, planning, and agent orchestration.
5. **Execution estate:** GBTSELF, HTMLSELF, local and distributed runtimes, tool bridges, and bounded actuation.
6. **Knowledge estate:** owned or lawfully controlled source data, knowledge graph, institutional memory, provenance, and retrieval.
7. **Qualification and assurance:** independent reproduction, adversarial evaluation, security review, dependency audit, and operational evidence.

### 5. Constitutional invariants

- Semantic force is not execution authority.
- Model output is a proposal, never an authorization.
- Network reachability is not identity or execution authority.
- Locality is not sovereignty.
- Access is not ownership.
- A thought is not authority.
- Observation is not authority.
- A receipt is not proof of a desired effect unless the effect was independently observed.
- Validity, authorization, admission, execution, and effect are distinct states.
- No production mutation occurs without explicit, bounded, evidence-bearing authorization.
- No missing evidence may be inferred as a successful qualification.
- External cognitive providers MUST be visible as explicit dependencies and MUST NOT be represented as OURSELF-originated cognition.

### 6. Alchemy epistemic discipline

Architecture and doctrine work MUST distinguish:

- **Established:** verified implementation fact, source-backed claim, or established engineering principle.
- **Well-Supported Interpretation:** interpretation supported by multiple verified facts or accepted mechanisms.
- **Plausible Inference:** reasoned conclusion whose premises are known but whose conclusion is not yet demonstrated.
- **Original Hypothesis:** newly proposed architecture or explanatory model requiring tests.
- **Speculative Extension:** exploratory possibility without sufficient evidence.
- **Unverified Claim:** claim for which adequate evidence is not currently available.

Symbolic, metaphysical, Kemetic, and alchemical language may serve as an interpretive layer. It MUST NOT substitute for technical mechanism, evidence, legal clarity, or measured results.

### 7. Sovereignty qualification record

Each qualification record MUST contain:

- reality and component identifier;
- claimed capability and bounded scope;
- owner/operator and authority boundary;
- source, model, data, and artifact provenance;
- dependency inventory, including network and service dependencies;
- custody evidence for code, data, model artifacts, and credentials;
- reproducible build/deployment procedure;
- independent test and evaluation results;
- security and failure-mode review;
- operational observation and tamper-evident receipt;
- unresolved limitations and external dependencies;
- qualification decision, reviewer, timestamp, and evidence references.

Permitted statuses:

- **UNASSESSED**
- **IN_DISCOVERY**
- **ENGINEERED_NOT_QUALIFIED**
- **QUALIFIED_WITH_DECLARED_DEPENDENCIES**
- **SOVEREIGN_WITHIN_DECLARED_SCOPE**
- **BLOCKED**
- **REVOKED**

A status MUST NOT be promoted without its required evidence.

### 8. Engineering sequence

1. Qualify OURSELF CONTROL and its identity, authority, jurisdiction, admission, and receipt boundaries.
2. Inventory and classify all current cognitive providers, runtimes, models, data paths, and credentials.
3. Establish an OURSELF-governed inference runtime with observable, reproducible operation.
4. Establish model artifact custody, tokenizer and architecture provenance, evaluation, and training lineage.
5. Build and evaluate OURSELF-originated model capabilities.
6. Integrate reasoning, memory, multimodality, and agent planning behind OURSELF control policies.
7. Qualify GBTSELF, HTMLSELF, and other execution realities independently.
8. Establish OURSELF KNOWLEDGE provenance, ownership or lawful control, retention, and reproducibility.
9. Conduct independent sovereignty qualification and publish evidence with explicit limits.

### 9. Lawful independent implementation

Reverse engineering means lawful study of observable behavior, protocols, public documentation, and independently obtained technical evidence, followed by clean-room or otherwise lawful independent implementation. It does not authorize theft, circumvention, misappropriation, or claims over third-party intellectual property.

### 10. Definition of completion

OURSELF OPENAI is not complete because a local model responds, a service is reachable, a UI is branded, or a roadmap exists.

A capability is complete only within a declared scope when OURSELF can demonstrate its authority, custody, operation, reproducibility, evaluation, dependency boundaries, and recovery without relying on an undisclosed external cognitive provider.

**Governing principle:** OURSELF is the authority over its own technological estate. Sovereignty is demonstrated by evidence, not asserted by naming.
